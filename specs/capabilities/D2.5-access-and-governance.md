# Capability 15 - Search, Access & Retrieval (SCH)

## 15.1 Purpose & scope
Finding and retrieving content and metadata: a **FHIR RESTful API** with search, version retrieval, and full-text/structured query, scoped by permissions (#17), for internal users, the authoring UI, and authorised downstream consumers (read side of the option-A boundary).

Out of scope: publication/distribution mechanics (#14); write/lifecycle (#7).

## 15.2 Actors & flows
Author, reviewer, publisher, auditor, downstream consumer. Flow: query by product/market/language/status/identifier/date -> permission-scoped results -> retrieve a version or the current-approved, with links to representations.

## 15.3 Functional requirements

| ID | Requirement | Pri |
|---|---|---|
| CAP-SCH-001 | Provide a FHIR RESTful API with search parameters (product, market, language, status, identifier, effective date). | M |
| CAP-SCH-002 | Retrieve a specific version and the current-approved version of a label per market/language. | M |
| CAP-SCH-003 | Provide full-text and structured search across content and metadata. | M |
| CAP-SCH-004 | Scope all results by caller permissions/attributes (#17); never leak out-of-scope content. | M |
| CAP-SCH-005 | Serve authorised downstream consumers read access to approved content (aligned to #14). | M |
| CAP-SCH-006 | Provide performance-bounded queries with pagination and result limits. | S |
| CAP-SCH-007 | Return links to available representations (FHIR/HTML/PDF) for a retrieved label. | S |
| CAP-SCH-008 | Expose an auditor/inspection-oriented retrieval path (with #19). | S |

## 15.4 Content & data model
Search index over content + metadata (product, market, language, status, dates, identifiers); API query model; representation links (to #13 outputs). Physical index technology decided in D3.

## 15.5 Standards & regulatory mapping
FHIR search framework and RESTful API; standard REST conventions. Downstream consumer access aligns to the option-A published contract (#14).

## 15.6 Lifecycle / owned state
None owned; read-only over content whose state is owned by #7.

## 15.7 Business rules & validation
Results are permission-scoped and attribute-filtered (#17); access to unpublished/draft content requires appropriate role/scope; current-approved resolution respects effective dating (#7/#14).

## 15.8 Interfaces
- **Exposes:** FHIR search/read API; retrieval endpoints.
- **Consumes:** #2 (content), #7 (versions/state), #17 (scoping), #18 (secure transport), #19 (audit of access where required).

## 15.9 Acceptance criteria / conformance
- A query returns only permission-scoped results; out-of-scope content is invisible.
- A specific version and the current-approved version are both retrievable.
- Access to draft/unpublished content is denied without the required scope.

## 15.10 Dependencies
#2, #7, #17, #18, #19.

# Capability 16 - Workflow & Approvals (WFL)

## 16.1 Purpose & scope
Configurable **review and approval routing**, invocation of **electronic signature** at approval gates (#19), and enforcement of **segregation of duties** (#17). Workflows drive lifecycle transitions in #7 and are reused by template approval (#3), variant/linguistic review (#9), and compliance sign-off (#12).

Out of scope: the signature mechanism/record itself (#19); lifecycle state ownership (#7).

## 16.2 Actors & flows
Author, reviewer, approver, publisher. Flow: route a draft through configured review/approval steps -> assign/escalate tasks -> invoke e-signature at approval -> enforce SoD -> trigger the corresponding #7 transition.

## 16.3 Functional requirements

| ID | Requirement | Pri |
|---|---|---|
| CAP-WFL-001 | Configurable multi-step review/approval workflows per market and label type (config-as-data, #21). | M |
| CAP-WFL-002 | Task assignment, reassignment, escalation, and due dates. | M |
| CAP-WFL-003 | Invoke electronic signature at approval gates (#19). | M |
| CAP-WFL-004 | Enforce segregation of duties via #17 (e.g. author cannot approve own content). | M |
| CAP-WFL-005 | Drive lifecycle transitions in #7 on step completion. | M |
| CAP-WFL-006 | Support sequential and parallel review paths and conditional routing. | S |
| CAP-WFL-007 | Notify participants of assignments, due dates, and outcomes (#20). | M |
| CAP-WFL-008 | Fully audit all workflow actions (#19). | M |

## 16.4 Content & data model
Workflow definition (steps, roles, conditions); workflow instance; tasks (assignee, status, due, escalation); links to the label version and the resulting #7 transition.

## 16.5 Standards & regulatory mapping
21 CFR Part 11 / EU Annex 11 (signing at approval, SoD); GxP review/approval practice.

## 16.6 Lifecycle / owned state
Owns **workflow instance and task states**; drives (does not own) label state in #7.

## 16.7 Business rules & validation
Approval requires a valid electronic signature (#19) and SoD (#17); steps complete in the defined order/conditions; overdue tasks escalate; a completed approval triggers exactly the permitted #7 transition.

## 16.8 Interfaces
- **Exposes:** workflow/task API; approval actions.
- **Consumes:** #17 (roles/SoD), #19 (e-signature + audit), #7 (transitions), #20 (notifications), #21 (workflow config); reused by #3, #9, #12.

## 16.9 Acceptance criteria / conformance
- A draft is routed and approved with a valid e-signature; SoD is enforced (author-approver blocked).
- Step completion triggers the correct #7 transition.
- Overdue tasks escalate; all actions are audited.

## 16.10 Dependencies
#7, #17, #19, #20, #21; reused by #3, #9, #12.

# Capability 17 - Identity, Access Control & Permissions - RBAC/ABAC (IAM)

## 17.1 Purpose & scope
Authentication/federation and combined **RBAC + ABAC** authorization, with permissions **scoped** across affiliate/organisation, region/market, product & label scope, lifecycle state, and template; functional roles; permission granting with **delegated, affiliate-scoped administration**; segregation of duties; and multi-tenant isolation of affiliate data. Foundational (P0) and enforced across every capability and API.

Out of scope: protection controls (#18); the audit record (#19).

## 17.2 Actors & flows
All users; administrator; delegated affiliate administrator. Flow: authenticate via enterprise IdP (SSO) -> authorize each action by role + attributes/scopes -> manage roles/permissions and delegated administration.

## 17.3 Functional requirements

| ID | Requirement | Pri |
|---|---|---|
| CAP-IAM-001 | Authenticate via the enterprise IdP (OIDC/SAML) with SSO/federation and MFA (delegated to IdP). | M |
| CAP-IAM-002 | Enforce combined RBAC + ABAC authorization on every action and API. | M |
| CAP-IAM-003 | Scope permissions across affiliate/organisation, region/market, product & label scope, lifecycle state, and template. | M |
| CAP-IAM-004 | Provide functional roles: author, reviewer, approver, publisher, template-owner, administrator, and auditor (read-only). | M |
| CAP-IAM-005 | Support permission granting with delegated, affiliate-scoped administration (a delegated admin cannot exceed their own scope). | M |
| CAP-IAM-006 | Enforce segregation of duties (e.g. author != approver) across workflows (#16). | M |
| CAP-IAM-007 | Enforce multi-tenant isolation of affiliate data. | M |
| CAP-IAM-008 | Provide central policy enforcement consumed uniformly by all capabilities. | M |
| CAP-IAM-009 | Record all access-control decisions and administration changes to audit (#19). | M |

## 17.4 Content & data model
Identity (from IdP); roles; permission scopes/attributes (affiliate, market, product/label, state, template); policies; delegation grants. Multi-tenancy/partition mapping coordinated with the D1 Section 6 decision.

## 17.5 Standards & regulatory mapping
OAuth2/OIDC/SAML; RBAC/ABAC; 21 CFR Part 11 (unique individual identities, SoD); Annex 11 access control.

## 17.6 Lifecycle / owned state
Owns access policy, roles, scopes, and delegation state.

## 17.7 Business rules & validation
Least privilege by default; SoD enforced; affiliate isolation enforced; a delegated administrator's grants are bounded by their own scope; every authorization decision is enforceable centrally and auditable.

## 17.8 Interfaces
- **Exposes:** authorization/policy decision service; role/permission administration.
- **Consumes:** enterprise IdP via #24; #19 (audit), #21 (policy config); **foundational to all capabilities**.

## 17.9 Acceptance criteria / conformance
- A user can see and act only within their role and scope; cross-affiliate access is denied.
- SoD blocks an author from approving their own content.
- A delegated affiliate admin cannot grant beyond their scope.
- All access decisions and admin changes are audited.

## 17.10 Dependencies
#24 (IdP), #19, #21; foundational to all capabilities.

# Capability 18 - Security (SEC)

## 18.1 Purpose & scope
Protection of data and services: encryption in transit and at rest, secrets management, network and application security, and vulnerability management. A cross-cutting control layer distinct from access control (#17) and audit (#19).

Out of scope: authorization (#17); the audit record (#19).

## 18.2 Actors & flows
Security/operations, platform, automated pipelines. Flow: protect data and communications; manage secrets and keys; secure APIs; manage vulnerabilities across the SDLC.

## 18.3 Functional requirements

| ID | Requirement | Pri |
|---|---|---|
| CAP-SEC-001 | Encrypt data in transit (TLS) and at rest. | M |
| CAP-SEC-002 | Manage secrets and cryptographic keys via a managed secrets/key store; no plaintext secrets. | M |
| CAP-SEC-003 | Apply network security (segmentation, gateway/WAF) and application security (input validation, OWASP Top 10 controls). | M |
| CAP-SEC-004 | Vulnerability and patch management, including dependency/container scanning. | M |
| CAP-SEC-005 | Follow a secure SDLC with security testing (SAST/DAST) integrated into CI/CD. | S |
| CAP-SEC-006 | Apply data-protection controls appropriate to the data held (access data, limited PII). | S |
| CAP-SEC-007 | Support security monitoring/alerting integrated with observability (#23) and audit (#19). | S |

## 18.4 Content & data model
Cross-cutting; no domain data owned. Governs secrets/keys, security configuration, and vulnerability records.

## 18.5 Standards & regulatory mapping
TLS; OWASP; ISO 27001-aligned controls; EU Annex 11 security expectations; GxP infrastructure qualification (with D3/CSV).

## 18.6 Lifecycle / owned state
No domain state owned; a control/enforcement layer.

## 18.7 Business rules & validation
Encryption is mandatory in transit and at rest; least-privilege network posture; no plaintext secrets; known high/critical vulnerabilities are tracked and remediated within policy.

## 18.8 Interfaces
- **Exposes:** security controls, secrets/key services, secure transport.
- **Consumes:** #17 (identity), #19 (audit), #23 (monitoring); protects all capabilities.

## 18.9 Acceptance criteria / conformance
- Data is encrypted in transit and at rest; secrets are vaulted.
- APIs pass security testing; high/critical vulnerabilities are tracked to remediation.
- Network segmentation and gateway controls are in place.

## 18.10 Dependencies
#17, #19, #23; protects all capabilities.

# Capability 19 - Audit Trail, e-Signature & Inspection Support (AUD)

## 19.1 Purpose & scope
The **evidentiary record**: a comprehensive, tamper-evident audit trail of all GxP-relevant actions; **electronic signatures** per 21 CFR Part 11 and EU Annex 11; full record-history reconstruction; **auditor/investigator** read-only inspection access; and audit-trail **search and visibility** for periodic review and inspections, with export/reporting for inspection support. It is the central audit sink every capability writes to.

Out of scope: authorization (#17); protection controls (#18); retention scheduling mechanics (#22, which #19 relies on for long-term retention).

## 19.2 Actors & flows
All capabilities (write audit); approver (signs via #16); auditor/inspector (read-only). Flow: capture audit events from every capability; apply e-signature at approval gates; provide inspection search, reconstruction, and export.

## 19.3 Functional requirements

| ID | Requirement | Pri |
|---|---|---|
| CAP-AUD-001 | Capture a comprehensive audit trail of all GxP-relevant actions (who, what, when, why, before/after values). | M |
| CAP-AUD-002 | Store audit records tamper-evidently/immutably (append-only). | M |
| CAP-AUD-003 | Provide electronic signatures per 21 CFR Part 11 & EU Annex 11: signing events, signature meaning/manifest, and binding to the signed record and signer identity. | M |
| CAP-AUD-004 | Reconstruct the full history of any record. | M |
| CAP-AUD-005 | Provide auditor/investigator access via a read-only inspection role and audit mode (#17). | M |
| CAP-AUD-006 | Provide audit-trail search, filtering, and visibility for periodic audit-trail review and regulatory inspections. | M |
| CAP-AUD-007 | Export and report audit content for inspection support. | M |
| CAP-AUD-008 | Receive audit events from every capability via the event backbone (#20) and direct writes. | M |
| CAP-AUD-009 | Apply retention and legal hold to audit records in coordination with #22. | M |

## 19.4 Content & data model
Audit event (actor, action, target, before/after, timestamp, reason/context); electronic-signature manifest (signer identity, meaning, timestamp, bound record hash); reconstruction views; retention/hold metadata (with #22). Aligns to ALCOA+ data-integrity principles.

## 19.5 Standards & regulatory mapping
21 CFR Part 11; EU Annex 11; ALCOA+ data integrity; GxP audit-trail review expectations.

## 19.6 Lifecycle / owned state
Owns audit records and electronic-signature records (immutable). Retention state is coordinated with #22.

## 19.7 Business rules & validation
Audit is append-only and immutable; a signature binds signer identity + meaning + record + timestamp; audit records are never deleted (only retained/disposed per #22 with legal hold); periodic audit-trail review is supported; inspection access is read-only and itself audited.

## 19.8 Interfaces
- **Exposes:** audit capture sink; e-signature service (to #16); inspection search/reconstruction/export.
- **Consumes:** #16 (signing invocation), #17 (identity/inspection role), #20 (event ingress), #22 (retention/hold), #21 (config); receives from all capabilities; feeds #23.

## 19.9 Acceptance criteria / conformance
- Every GxP-relevant action is captured immutably with before/after values.
- Electronic signatures meet Part 11/Annex 11 and bind identity, meaning, and record.
- An auditor can search, reconstruct a record's history, and export for inspection - read-only.
- Audit records are retained per schedule and honour legal hold.

## 19.10 Dependencies
#16, #17, #20, #22, #21; receives audit from all capabilities; feeds #23.
